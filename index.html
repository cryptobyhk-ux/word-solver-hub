Frequently Asked Questions

Is WordSolver Hub really free?

Yes. Every solver and game on this site is free, with no sign-in, no download, and no paywall.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play and use every tool right away. Scores are saved only in your own browser if you choose to track them.

Are the daily puzzles the same for everyone?

Yes. Daily puzzles are picked from the calendar date in US Eastern time, so every player in the United States sees the same daily puzzle on the same day.
