Privacy Policy

WordSolver Hub is designed to collect as little as possible.

What We Collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name or email to play or to use a solver. Solver input and game play happen in your browser and are not sent to our server.

Local Storage

If you play a game, your score may be saved in your own browser's local storage, on the Leaderboards page. You can clear those scores at any time from that page. Clearing your browser data also clears them.

Cookies and Ads

This launch version sets no tracking cookies and shows no third-party ads. If ads or analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they go live.

Children

WordSolver Hub is a general word game site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
