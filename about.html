About WordSolver Hub

WordSolver Hub is an original, free website for word game players in the United States. We build word solvers, daily word games, logic puzzles, and number games that run entirely in your browser.

Our solvers use a curated list of common US English words. Our daily puzzles are picked from the US Eastern calendar date, so every player sees the same daily puzzle on the same day. Our guides are written in plain US English, with rules first and strategy second.

We do not copy other sites. All code, text, puzzles, and word lists on this site were written for WordSolver Hub. Game names here are generic descriptions, not trademarks of any publisher, and we do not publish answers for other publishers' daily puzzles.

There is no account to create, no download to install, and no paywall on results. If you want to track scores, they are saved only in your own browser.
